# DeSci Lab

**Verifiable open science pipeline** — AI-powered literature scanning, sourced hypotheses, community peer review, and on-chain funding.

## What we do
DeSci Lab is an on-chain AI co-scientist that:
1. Scans peer-reviewed literature across longevity, neuroplasticity, and climate biotech
2. Generates sourced, falsifiable hypotheses with full citation chains
3. Routes top hypotheses to community expert review
4. Prepares validated ideas for on-chain grant funding — with auditable receipts at every step
