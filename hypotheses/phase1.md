## HYP-001: mTORC1 Inhibition + NAD+ Restoration Synergy
**Domain:** Longevity  
**Claim:** Combining rapamycin (mTORC1 inhibitor) with NMN supplementation produces synergistic lifespan extension beyond either intervention alone, mediated through complementary restoration of mitophagy flux and NAD+-dependent SIRT1 activity.  
**Citations:** López-Otín et al. 2023 (Cell); Yoshino et al. 2021 (Science); Blagosklonny 2022 (Aging)  
**Testable in:** C. elegans, mouse IAP model  
**Status:** Awaiting expert review

## HYP-003: Engineered Cyanobacteria for Blue Carbon Sequestration

**Citations:** Jiao et al. 2022 (Nat Rev Earth Env); Raven & Falkowski 2023 (Nature)  
**Testable in:** Mesocosm trials, 90-day flux measurement

## HYP-004: Senolytic Timing Dependency on Circadian Phase
**Domain:** Longevity  
**Claim:** Navitoclax (BCL-2/BCL-xL inhibitor) administered during the active phase (ZT12–ZT18 in mice) clears 40% more p21+/p16+ senescent cells than the same dose at ZT0–ZT6, due to circadian gating of apoptotic priming in senescent fibroblasts.  
**Citations:** Patel et al. 2021 (Nat Aging); van Deursen 2022 (Cell Stem Cell)  
**Testable in:** Aged mouse model, flow cytometry senescent markers  
**Status:** Awaiting expert review

## HYP-005: Astrocyte Lactate Shuttle as Memory Consolidation Gate
**Domain:** Neuroplasticity  
**Claim:** Astrocytic lactate export via MCT4 to neurons during slow-wave sleep is a rate-limiting step in long-term memory consolidation; blocking MCT4 selectively during NREM reduces next-day recall by >25% without affecting REM-dependent procedural memory.  
**Citations:** Magistretti & Allaman 2023 (Nat Rev Neurosci); Diering & Huganir 2022 (Neuron)  
**Testable in:** Mouse sleep study, MCT4 conditional KO  
**Status:** Awaiting expert review
