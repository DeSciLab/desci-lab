# VitaDAO Proposal (DRAFT) — Decoupling Taurine's Geroprotection from Baseline Status

**Linked hypothesis:** HYP-007

## One-line ask

A small, mechanistic validation grant to test whether taurine's healthspan benefit is routed through the **NLRP3 inflammasome** and is **independent of baseline taurine status** — resolving a live contradiction in the longevity literature before larger trials misallocate capital.

## Problem

The taurine–aging field is split: the 2023 *Science* lifespan result framed taurine as a declining factor to restore, but 2024–2026 work shows circulating taurine doesn't reliably decline with age, while a 2026 *Cell* study shows supplementation still helps *replete* old mice via NLRP3. Trials are being designed around a biomarker (baseline taurine) that may not predict response. That's a capital-efficiency problem DeSci can de-risk cheaply.

## Proposed experiment (minimal, decisive)

1. **Aged mouse cohort**, stratified by baseline circulating taurine (high vs. low).
2. **Arms:** taurine vs. vehicle, in WT and *Nlrp3*-KO backgrounds.
3. **Primary readouts:** IL-1β / NLRP3 activation markers; healthspan battery (grip strength, glucose tolerance, coordination).
4. **Decision rule:**
   - Benefit correlates with NLRP3/IL-1β suppression but **not** baseline deficiency → HYP-007 supported; reframe trial inclusion criteria.
   - Benefit disappears in *Nlrp3*-KO → mechanism confirmed as inflammasome-gated.
